How I workThree habits I bring to every project

Look closely.
Check the numbers.
Say it simply.

  1. 01

    Start with the people involved

    Before I form an opinion, I try to understand who the problem affects. Talking to delivery partners for my ethnography project changed what I thought the real issues were.

  2. 02

    Put numbers to the question

    Where I can, I turn an idea into something I can measure, like modelling a café’s costs and revenue to see exactly where it starts making money.

  3. 03

    Make it easy to follow

    A finding only helps if others understand it. I like shaping research into a clear report, video or presentation that a reader can act on.
